From catalogue to confirmed order.

Three steps. The boring parts, automated.

  1. 01

    Browse varieties

    Pick from five graded varieties. Each card carries the ASTA colour, the SHU heat, and the rate for your quantity band.

  2. 02

    Get your quote

    Enter the quantity, the app fills the right tier, you adjust if needed, and a PDF quotation is generated on the spot.

  3. 03

    Confirm and ship

    Send the quotation over email or WhatsApp, log the confirmation, and we move the order to dispatch.

What the app does, in one screen.

Less spreadsheet. More shipping.

Inbox

Gmail auto-import

IndiaMART enquiries land in your inbox. The app reads them, extracts the buyer, the variety, and the quantity, and files them in the leads list. No copy-paste.

Parsing

AI enquiry parser

Free-text enquiries get parsed into structured leads. Variety, quantity, location, and buyer name drop into the right fields, ready to quote.

Quoting

PDF quotations

Generate a clean, branded PDF in one click. Quantity, tier, line total, and your contact details are already laid out.

Pricing

Quantity-tiered rates

Every variety supports three price bands. The quote page picks the right rate the moment a quantity is entered, and you can override.

Pipeline

Follow-up scheduling

Every lead gets a next follow-up date. The dashboard surfaces what is due today and what is overdue.
